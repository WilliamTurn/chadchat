# The composition canon — index (2026-07-23)

**This is the living copy** (ported via D2, 2026-07-23). The copy at `chadlatest/audits/design-standards-2026-07-23/composition-canon/` is the frozen research artifact; edits land here only.

The explicit, enumerated body of screen-composition decision rules for building this product's surfaces, researched from the canonical sources (Refactoring UI, NN/g and the Gestalt literature, Apple HIG, Material 3, GOV.UK, Baymard, Polaris, Carbon, ADS, Vercel WIG, Every Layout, Müller-Brockmann, Luke Wroblewski, Palmer 1992) by an eight-agent research team, then reconciled by an adversarial completeness sweep and patched in place 2026-07-23. Every principle: one bolded imperative rule, a plain-English explanation, its source(s), and a tag — [mechanical] (a lint or test can check it) or [judgment] (an auditor must look). Every rule is conditional — a test you run — never a blanket ban or blanket permission.

**How this canon relates to the ux-canon and the north star:** this is the ASSEMBLY layer. The ds.css tokens (APPROVED, owner 2026-07-17) say what the atoms are; the ux-canon (`audits/workout-overhaul-2026-07-22/ux-canon/`, 1,311 principles) says how each element behaves and renders; this canon says how elements assemble into screens — grouping, separation, density, placement, recipes, reflow. On any conflict: owner decisions and the north star (`design-north-star-2026-07-16.md`) win over this canon; the APPROVED ds.css tokens win over any canon arithmetic (the 2026-07-23 patch re-anchored all numbers to them); this canon defers element-level law to the ux-canon by cross-reference and never restates it.

Three rules are stated exactly once and cited everywhere else: the separation ladder (01 #11, rungs 0–8), the gap table (06 #8, `--sp` tiers + `--zone-gap`, ≥2× floor), and the horizontal-inset budget (02 #13, `--gutter` + at most one additional inset).
