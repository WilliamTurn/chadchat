## (a) Method

1. Read all eight files in full (every rule, every contested section, every sourcing note).
2. Assembled independent maps of the screen-composition field:
   - **Refactoring UI** layout/hierarchy chapter structure and **Tidwell, *Designing Interfaces* 3rd ed.** Ch4 (Layout) + Ch6 (Mobile) pattern lists — training knowledge (both stable, unversioned texts).
   - **Every Layout** primitives — **fetched live 2026-07-23**: Stack, Box, Center, Cluster, Sidebar, Switcher, Cover, Grid, Frame, Reel, Imposter, Icon, Container.
   - **Polaris patterns index** — **fetched live 2026-07-23**: app settings layout, card layout, resource details layout, resource index layout, common actions, date picking, loading states (archived), badges.
   - **Material 3** foundations→layout tree (understanding/applying/canonical layouts, window size classes, parts of layout) and **component-anatomy index** — m3.material.io is JS-rendered and 404'd/unfetchable this session (same result as both precedent sweeps); used training knowledge corroborated by the canon files' own July-2026 verifications (07's window-class check, 08's anatomy check).
   - **Apple HIG** Layout / Presentation sections (boxes, layout, modality, sheets, popovers) — training knowledge; the canon files individually re-verified the HIG pages they cite.
   - **Carbon** layout guidance (2x grid, spacing, aspect ratios) and **Baymard page-type taxonomy** (homepage, product list, product page, cart/checkout, accounts, mobile) — training knowledge plus the canon files' cited public summaries.
   - **Product simulation**: walked this product's actual and decided surfaces (Home per the 2026-07-20 IA decision, category pages, workout runner, workout-complete, history, stats, settings, chat, onboarding, paywall/locked tier states) and asked per surface and per screen furniture item: *which composition file owns this?*
3. Cross-checked every seam named in the brief plus every pair I found myself, and every canon rule that presumes a ds.css token, against `app/design-system/ds.css` (read this session), the north star, the wave0 decision log (read in full), ux-canon 04 (headings + §14 read), and ux-canon 02 §17 (read).

Severity key: **MISSING** = no file owns it and a session building this product would need it. **THIN** = mentioned but not decision-grade. **DELIBERATE-EXCLUSION-OK** = absent and defensible.

Verdict up front, because a sweep that finds nothing is suspect: the set is strong at its centers — the container-earning tests (01 §4), the overlay anatomy (04), the recipes (05), and the responsive invariants (07 §A) are build-from-able and better-sourced than most public systems. But the eight files **do not agree with each other on the numbers** (four different spacing/ratio systems, two incompatible separation ladders, a horizontal budget that its own sibling rules and the approved ds.css tokens both violate), and screen furniture that sits BETWEEN zones — banners, toasts, z-order, selection modes — fell between all eight domains. **4 MISSING, 10 THIN, 11 contradictions, 7 house conflicts.**

## (e) Contradictions (11) — file+rule numbers and proposed resolutions

The brief's suspicion was correct: the parallel authors built overlapping number systems that disagree. C1–C6 are the load-bearing ones.

1. **Two separation ladders with incompatible rungs and semantics.** 01 #11: nine rungs (0 flow, 1 whitespace, 2 alignment shift, 3 header, 4 divider, 5 tint, 6 border, 7 shadow, 8 separate surface). 06 #1: six rungs (a whitespace, b +header, c +divider, d +tint, e +atmosphere shift, f container) — **cumulative** ("each rung below assumes the ones above it are already in play"), no alignment rung, an atmosphere rung 01 doesn't have, no elevation or separate-surface rung. The cumulative semantics of 06's ladder (rung c = whitespace + header + divider stacked on one boundary) collides head-on with 01 #14, 02 #37, and 06's own #4 (one marker per boundary; header+space is the only sanctioned pair). Cross-file references to "rung 5/6" vs "rung d/f" cannot be resolved by a reader. **Resolution:** declare 01 #11 the canonical ladder (it is the more complete and the more carefully argued); rewrite 06 §1 as the zone-level view of the SAME ladder using 01's numbering, insert alignment as a rung, place "atmosphere shift" as a named variant of rung 5 (a full-bleed tint whose hue carries zone identity — 06 #38–39 already define it), and delete the cumulative framing.
2. **Divider between unlike zones: permitted vs forbidden.** 01 #28 (M3): "full-width dividers separate larger unrelated regions." 06 #22: "Between two unlike zones, a divider is the wrong tool — that seam belongs to the whitespace beat and header grammar." **Resolution:** 06's position is the house position (north star: dividers are a dense-context substitute, per 02 #37); amend 01 #28 to scope full-width dividers to *within-surface* group closure (its own #26 already pushes this) and cite 06 #22 for zone seams.
3. **The horizontal budget outlaws its own siblings and the approved token.** 02 #13 caps ALL left insets (margin + padding + indent) at ≤~24px on phones, assuming a 16–20px margin + 4–8px more. But: (a) 02 #15 permits a 12–16px structural indent — over its own remaining budget; (b) 01 #59 treats a 16px-padded earned card inside 16px margins (32px inset) as expensive-but-legal, and 01 §4 explicitly legitimizes earned containers on phones — which 02 #13 arithmetically bans; (c) **ds.css `--gutter: clamp(1.5rem, 6vw, 3rem)` is 24px minimum at phone widths** — the approved token alone consumes 02 #13's entire budget, and 07 #29's "16px is the industry default" doesn't match the token either. **Resolution:** restate 02 #13 as token-relative — "beyond `--gutter`, at most ONE additional inset (one indent level OR one earned container's padding), never stacked" — which preserves its true target (cards-in-cards, 16+16+16) without banning earned containment or contradicting the DS. Fix 07 #29 and 01 #59's example numbers to cite `--gutter`. (Also house conflict H2.)
4. **Indent depth and step size.** 02 #15: cap structural indent at ONE level (~12–16px) on phones. 06 #28: one fixed step "e.g. 16 or 24px," and "two indent levels deep is the practical maximum on a phone." **Resolution:** one level as the default, two as the hard max (aligns with ux-canon 04 #36's nesting cap); one step token (16px) named in both rules.
5. **Four incompatible gap-band systems, while 06 #9 demands disjoint bands.** Within/group/zone gaps: 02 #20 = 4–12 / 16–24 / **32–48+**; 06 #8 = 8–12 / 16–24 / **48–64 phone, ≤96 desktop**; 01 #22 = groups at **24–32**; ds.css `--zone-gap` = **56–96**. A 32px gap is a zone gap in 02, a nothing in 06, and a group gap in 01. **Resolution:** one table, token-anchored: within `--sp-2/3` (8–12), group `--sp-4/5` (16–24), zone `--zone-gap` (56–96) — matching the approved token and 06; correct 02 #20's 32–48 and 01 #22's 24–32 to it.
6. **Header vertical budget vs header spacing grammar.** 02 #23: an in-page header "plus its gaps spends ≤ ~48px of vertical total." 06 #14: space-above ALONE ≈ the zone beat (48–64), space-below 12–16. **Resolution:** 06 #14's asymmetry is the real rule; 02 #23's budget should exclude the zone gap above (which is the zone boundary's cost, not the header's) — restate as "header line + below-gap ≤ ~48px."
7. **Sticky-chrome cap: two numbers.** 02 #29: ~25% of viewport. 07 #33: "together well under a third." Both self-describe as heuristics. **Resolution:** pick 25% (the stricter, and 02's is [mechanical]); 07 #33 cites it.
8. **Form action alignment: left-anchored vs trailing.** 03 #40: form buttons anchor to the form's LEFT content edge, primary first (GOV.UK). 08 #13: "in a horizontal action pair, the primary takes the trailing (right) position… (wizard footers, inline confirms, editor bars)" — wizard footers ARE forms. 08's contested section defers to 03, and 03 did decide — but 08 #13's scope list re-captures the deferred case, so a builder gets both answers. Also unstated boundary with 08 #12 (desktop page-header trailing primary). **Resolution:** amend 08 #13's scope to "dialog/overlay and inline-confirm pairs (per comp-canon 04 §28)"; forms and wizard steps follow 03 #40 (left edge, primary first); add the cross-ref both ways. One convention per context, stated once.
9. **Short-field rows: never stack vs stack when narrow.** 03 #23: min/s and date rows "do not restack at phone widths… stacking makes it worse," because 03 #18 requires them designed to fit 320px. 04 §46: label+field+unit rows "either fit with flexible field widths or wrap label-above-field… let the group stack when the column is narrow" — for the SAME duration-row case both files cite as the motivating defect. **Resolution:** 03's fit-by-design is primary (short fixed fields must fit 320 and never stack); 04 §46's stacking is the fallback for rows containing *variable-width* fields only. State the split in both rules.
10. **The between/within ratio is stated four ways.** 05 #3: "1.5–2×" workable default. 06 #8: "at minimum ~2×… the ≥2× jump is not [tunable]." 02 #35: ≥2× zone-level, ≥1.5–2× group-level. 01 #22: "at least two steps on the spacing scale" (≈2× on the 4px scale). **Resolution:** adopt 06 #8's ≥2× as the mechanical floor at every level (01's scale-step form is the implementation of it); correct 05 #3 and 02 #35's 1.5× low end.
11. **Desktop form-column placement.** 03 #38: the column "left-aligns with the page's content edge… it does not center-stretch." 05 #55: "on desktop, a centered readable column." 07 #18: "centers or left-anchors." **Resolution:** left-anchor to the page grid when the form lives inside an app surface (03's case, the house default per 06 #26's spine); centered only when the whole screen is a centered composition (onboarding/celebration — 02 #44 already draws exactly this line). State it once in 03 #38 and cite from 05/07.

**Checked and consistent (no action):** 02 fold rules vs 05 #4–5 fold discipline vs 01 #30 false floors (aligned, including the peek-past-the-fold rule); 01 #45 overlay-content-flat vs 04 §16–21 (aligned, same motivating screenshot); 01 #32 floating-tint-is-a-card vs 06 #24 (aligned); 01 #28 vs 06 #21 inset-divider semantics (aligned); 05's recipes vs 01's earning tests — no recipe prescribes an unearned container (05 #13/#26 ban them; chat user-bubbles pass provenance #41; onboarding option tiles pass single-target #39; chips pass #39/#40 — though 05 #72 and #80 should CITE the tests, see work-order); 08 #13 vs 04 §28 dialog actions (aligned via deferral); divider opacity 06 #20 (8–15%) vs ds.css `--hairline`(.09)/`--hairline-2`(.16) (top token 1pt over the stated band — tolerable, note it); 03 #40 phone full-width buttons vs 07 #14 (aligned).
