# Information architecture, navigation, and flows (canon 02)

Owning file: `docs/ux-canon/02-navigation-ia-flows.md` (189 principles). **[M]** mechanical, **[J]** judgment. Cite numbers (e.g. `// canon 02 §57`).

## Hard-rule digest

**1. IA & hierarchy** — 1 [J] structure around user tasks, not org/schema. 2 [J] broad-and-shallow over narrow-and-deep. 3 [J] near the entry, but never sacrifice scent to a click count. 4 [J] one obvious home per item; cross-link, don't duplicate. 5 [J] group by user meaning. 6 [J] top-level categories mutually exclusive. 7 [M] 5–8 top-level destinations max (Material nav bar 3–5). 8 [J] expose the structure. 9 [M] tree-test/card-sort before big reorganizations. 10 [J] design for the 80% task; long tail behind More/search. 11 [J] restructure when growing, don't append. 12 [J] Home orients (what/what-can-I-do/what-needs-me-now).

**2. Navigation patterns** — 13 [M] 3–5 coequal top destinations = bottom tab bar. 14 [M] tabs persist across main screens. 15 [M] tap current tab again → its root / scroll top. 16 [M] each tab keeps its own stack; switching preserves position. 17 [M] never use a tab as an action button. 18 [M] tab icons get labels. 19 [M] don't hide primary nav behind a hamburger when there's space. 20 [M] desktop exposes nav as top bar or side rail. 21 [J] hub-and-spoke only when spokes don't interconnect. 22 [J] wizard nav for linear one-off tasks only. 23 [J] drawer only for secondary/overflow, cost known. 24 [M] in-page tabs/segmented controls subdivide a screen, never replace app nav. 25 [M] nav placement/order/contents identical everywhere (no adaptive reordering). 26 [M] indicate current location in the nav. 27 [M] primary actions & nav in the thumb zone. 28 [M] gestural nav supplements visible controls. 29 [J] don't invent novel nav when a standard fits. 30 [M] escape from every screen — no dead ends.

**3. Labeling & wayfinding** — 31 [J] front-loaded specific nouns users would say. 32 [M] one name per concept everywhere (nav/title/link/breadcrumb match). 33 [M] screen title matches the label tapped to reach it. 34 [J] avoid catch-alls (More/Other/Tools) for findable things. 35 [M] icons never carry meaning alone (bar the universal few). 36 [J] every screen answers where-am-I/what's-here/where-can-I-go. 37 [M] breadcrumbs on hierarchies >2 levels. 38 [M] breadcrumbs reflect hierarchy, not history. 39 [M] confirm state changes in place (active filter chips, counts). 40 [M] empty sections still orient. 41 [J] user's language for section names.

**4. Back & history** — 42 [M] browser back always works and goes where expected. 43 [J] each perceived "new place" gets a history entry; transient states don't. 44 [M] back never loses work (persist or confirm; don't confirm when nothing's at stake). 45 [M] back restores scroll/filters/data, not just the URL. 46 [M] in-app back navigates up/back within the app, never exits. 47 [M] distinguish back (history) from up (hierarchy); deep-linked entries get a sane up-path. 48 [M] closing a modal/sheet returns to the exact underlying context. 49 [M] no history spam (replace intermediate redirects). 50 [M] multi-step back is per-step + post-completion back never resubmits (PRG).51 [M] support platform back gestures everywhere.

**5. URL & deep-link state** — 52 [M] every perceivable place has a stable shareable URL/deep link. 53 [M] reflect view-defining state in the URL (tab/filters/sort/query/pagination/selection); refresh restores exactly. 54 [M] don't put transient/private state in the URL. 55 [J] filters/sorts use replace while refining; discrete navigations push. 56 [J] readable hierarchical URLs. 57 [M] deep links survive auth (login → originally requested screen). 58 [M] deep links build proper surrounding context (up-nav, correct tab). 59 [M] old URLs redirect; removed content → helpful navigable 404. 60 [M] navigation uses real `<a>` links. 61 [M] gone/inaccessible deep-linked views degrade gracefully.

**6. Search** — 62 [J] search supplements good IA. 63 [M] search where users look, reachable from relevant screens. 64 [M] state the scope. 65 [M] recents + as-you-type suggestions. 66 [M] autocomplete keyboard-navigable, capped, never traps focus. 67 [M] tolerate typos/synonyms/plurals/diacritics. 68 [M] results page shows query (editable) + count + scope. 69 [M] no-results is a recovery tool, never a dead end. 70 [M] active filters visible + individually removable + clear-all. 71 [M] search state shareable/restorable. 72 [J] rank by usefulness; show why a result matched.

**7. Onboarding & first-run** — 73 [J] minimize time-to-first-value. 74 [J] progressive/contextual over front-loaded tours. 75 [M] every step skippable and safe. 76 [J] let people try before signup where possible. 77 [J] personalization questions only when they visibly change the experience, and say so. 78 [J] defer every deferrable ask. 79 [J] design the empty first-day deliberately. 80 [J] action-driven teaching. 81 [M] never re-onboard a returning user. 82 [M] coach marks one at a time, anchored, dismiss on interaction. 83 [M] instrument the funnel.

**8. Multi-step flows** — 84 [J] focused steps, one job per screen. 85 [M] show progress (no tracker for 2-step flows). 86 [J] order easy→invested. 87 [M] every step back-navigable without data loss (forward too). 88 [M] long/high-stakes flows save + resume. 89 [M] review step before irreversible submission (per-item Change links). 90 [M] explicit confirmation screen after submission (what happened / next / receipt / onward). 91 [M] validate per step at the right moment. 92 [J] don't ask for what you don't need/have/can-derive. 93 [J] modal wizard only for short atomic flows; long flows get full-screen URLs per step. 94 [J] branch instead of showing irrelevant steps. 95 [M] primary Continue dominant, consistent, disable-free.

**9. Progressive disclosure** — 96 [J] common case first; advanced on demand. 97 [M] the reveal control is visible/labeled/hints at what it hides. 98 [J] one or two layers, not mazes. 99 [J] staged (flows) vs progressive (options). 100 [M] never hide info needed for the current decision (price/consequences/requirements). 101 [J] remember disclosure state where it helps.

**10. Settings** — 102 [J] predictable groups (Account/Notifications/Appearance/Privacy/Support). 103 [J] order by frequency-of-need. 104 [M] searchable once >~2 screens. 105 [M] each setting states its effect, shows current value, applies immediately (or says when). 106 [M] separate the danger zone. 107 [M] account deletion exists, is findable in-app, and actually deletes. 108 [M] settings never require knowing a "save" existed (autosave or unmissable save bar). 109 [J] don't bury features in settings or leak settings into the product. 110 [M] every notification/email is individually controllable from one screen.

**11. Account & auth** — 111 [M] signup asks the minimum. 112 [M] platform accelerators (Apple/Google/passkeys) + short list. 113 [M] password rules up front, live-check, paste+reveal. 114 [M] distinguish login from signup; catch crossover errors ("email already has an account"). 115 [M] password reset is first-class. 116 [M] login errors don't over-reveal but say what to do next. 117 [J] keep users logged in; step-up auth for sensitive actions. 118 [M] session expiry mid-task preserves work + returns after re-auth. 119 [M] biometric unlock as opt-in accelerator. 120 [M] logout findable, immediate, scoped. 121 [J] email verification doesn't block first value unless truly required.

**12. Subscription/paywall/checkout (ethics)** — 122 [M] full price/period/renewal before any commitment control. 123 [M] trials state length/end/charge + notify before conversion. 124 [M] cancellation as easy as signup, same channel. 125 [M] one save-attempt max on cancel, honestly framed (no confirm-shaming). 126 [J] scannable plan comparison, honest anchoring. 127 [J] paywall states free-vs-paid truthfully, never mid-task trap. 128 [M] offer guest checkout; never force account to buy. 129 [M] enclose checkout, persistent order summary. 130 [M] show shipping/tax/fees early. 131 [M] platform payment accelerators + card form conventions. 132 [M] no pre-checked add-ons/sneaked items/fake timers. 133 [J] self-serve refunds/plan-changes/pauses.

**13. Permissions** — 134 [M] never ask at launch en masse. 135 [M] ask in context, triggered by the user's action. 136 [J] prime before the one-shot system prompt. 137 [J] explain benefit, not mechanism. 138 [M] request minimum scope, escalate later. 139 [M] denial is a supported path, no nag loop. 140 [M] previously-denied → explain + deep-link to settings. 141 [M] never block the whole app on a non-essential permission.

**14. Notifications strategy** — 142 [J] every notification worth an interruption (user value). 143 [M] ask permission only after demonstrating value. 144 [M] per-category controls, not one master switch. 145 [J] batch/digest low-urgency. 146 [M] every notification deep-links to the exact screen. 147 [M] truthful, self-contained, no bait/fake urgency. 148 [M] in-app state + notifications stay consistent (clear badge on action). 149 [M] opt-out one tap from the message. 150 [J] choose channel by urgency/locus.

**15. Cross-device / multi-tab** — 151 [M] signed-in data current on every device without manual sync. 152 [J] sync conflicts resolve without data loss. 153 [J] in-progress state syncs, not just committed data. 154 [M] the web app behaves in multiple tabs (auth changes detected). 155 [J] responsive is one product, not two. 156 [M] show sync status only when it's news. 157 [M] deep links/emails/notifications work regardless of device.

**16. Interruptions & resumability** — 158 [M] assume every session ends mid-task; persist continuously. 159 [M] restore to where the user was on return. 160 [J] if restoration is stale, offer resume rather than force. 161 [M] warn before discarding only when there's something to discard. 162 [M] long operations survive backgrounding/navigation. 163 [M] timed processes show the clock + offer extension. 164 [J] app-caused interruptions are the app's to repair. 165 [J] offline is an interruption, not an error.

**17. Modality & overlay containers** — 166 [J] choose the container by task weight: popover/menu (one glance) → dialog (one decision) → bottom sheet (mobile option sets/short sub-tasks) → side panel (desktop context) → full screen+URL (multi-step/resumable). 167 [J] blocking modality only when the task must complete/abandon first. 168 [M] bottom sheets follow the contract (handle, detents, drag-down/scrim dismiss, inner scroll at full height). 169 [M] gesture-dismissible surfaces also dismiss by visible control; dirty content guarded. 170 [J] never stack transient surfaces >1 deep. 171 [M] same task = same container at the same breakpoint, adapted as a designed pair.

**18. Scheduling views** — 172 [J] default to the user's planning granularity; the chosen view persists. 173 [M] Today always marked and one tap away. 174 [M] scheduled items show state (upcoming/done/missed) without color alone. 175 [M] rescheduling is direct (drag + explicit "move to"); recurring asks scope.

**19. Transactional email** — 176 [M] one job, one CTA, single column. 177 [M] specific subject + consistent product sender name. 178 [M] survive hostile clients (real text, plain-text part, no image-only, real-link buttons). 179 [M] say why they got it + what happens if ignored. 180 [M] time-limited links state expiry; expired-link page offers a fresh one. 181 [M] marketing and transactional are separate streams.

**20. Notification inbox** — 182 [M] the inbox is the durable record of transient channels. 183 [M] per-item unread, drives the bell badge, clears on view, mark-all-read one tap. 184 [M] each item deep-links + reads as one scannable line; group repeats. 185 [J] not a marketing surface, doesn't accumulate forever.

**21. Guest→account migration** — 186 [M] everything a guest created survives account creation (merge, never discard). 187 [M] say what happens to guest data at the signup moment. 188 [M] signing into an existing account reconciles safely (merge or ask). 189 [M] guest limits stated where they bind, before work is invested.
