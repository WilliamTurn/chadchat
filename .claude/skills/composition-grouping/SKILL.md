# Containers, rhythm, and density (composition canon 01 + 06 + 02)

Owning files: `docs/composition-canon/01-container-grouping.md` (83 principles), `06-rhythm-sectioning.md` (50), `02-density-breathing-room.md` (54). **[M]** mechanical, **[J]** judgment. Cite ids (e.g. `// comp-canon 01 #38`). This is the ASSEMBLY layer: it composes UNDER owner decisions + the north star, and UNDER the `app/globals.css` tokens (ds.css port pending — re-point on land); element-level law stays in the ux-canon and is cross-referenced, never restated. ## Hard-rule digest — canon 01 (container and grouping logic)

**§1 Gestalt baseline** — 1 [J] exhaust invisible grouping first: proximity, alignment, similarity, continuity. 2 [J] move closer before enclosing. 3 [J] shared edges = membership, indents = subordination. 4 [J] style identically what is the same kind; styling difference is a claim of meaning. 5 [J] series content on one continuous line/column. 6 [J] common region is the strongest force — ration it. 7 [J] connecting lines only for genuinely connective content (steppers, timelines). 8 [J] audit what whitespace accidentally claims. 9 [J] the page surface is itself a common region. 10 [M] verify grouping with a squint/blur pass.

**§2 The separation ladder** — 11 [M] THE ladder, weakest→strongest: (0) shared flow · (1) whitespace step · (2) alignment shift/indent · (3) section header · (4) hairline divider · (5) tint zone · (6) bordered container · (7) elevated card · (8) separate surface; each rung substitutes for those below, never adds. 12 [J] use the weakest rung that makes the boundary unambiguous. 13 [J] escalate one rung at a time, reason per step. 14 [J] one boundary marker per boundary (header+divider+tint+border = same thing 4×; sanctioned pair = name + separator). 15 [J] torn between rungs → take the lower; over-separation is contagious. 16 [M] N groups need at most N−1 boundaries; ubiquitous separators say nothing. 17 [J] pick rung by semantic distance, not content volume. 18 [J] rungs 6+ claim objecthood ("tap me") — never use one for mere separation. 19 [J] rung 8 for content that can't share context; never a mini-dialog as a card, never exile to overlay to dodge layout. 20 [M] scroll-surviving boundaries = sticky header + on-scroll shadow, not heavier chrome.

**§3 Rules per rung** — 21 [J] whitespace suffices for ~2–6 internally-tight groups (north-star default). 22 [M] between-group space ≥2 scale steps above within-group (gap table 06 #8). 23 [J] indent/edge change for sub-parts of one flow. 24 [J] header only when the group needs a NAME; self-labeling content gets none. 25 [J] titled whitespace group ≈ titled card — substitute first. 26 [J] divider where space can't be afforded or the eye recrosses (lists, menus, tables); space first. 27 [M] dividers hairline, low-contrast, tokenized. 28 [M] inset = group continues, full-width = group ends, within one surface only; between unlike zones a divider is wrong. 29 [J] skip dividers under ~5 well-spaced siblings. 30 [J] no full-width divider near the fold (false floor). 31 [J] tint zone = different in KIND, not an object. 32 [J] tint bleeds to a screen edge; floating rounded tint = borderless card = rung 6. 33 [M] one tint level between page and content; never stack tints. 34 [J] zebra striping for wide dense tables only. 35 [M] earned borders are hairline; padding exceeds visual weight. 36 [M] shadows only for unit-interactive or floating containment; static info never elevates. 37 [M] radius from tokens, concentric math; needing radius arithmetic = re-check depth.

**§4 The six earning tests (a group earns rung 6+ ONLY by passing one)** — 38 [M] "it's a section of content" is NOT a test; the tests: 39 [M] single-target (whole group is one tap target). 40 [J] collection-member (selectable/comparable/individually-actionable repeating items; scan-only homogeneous feeds stay flat rows — owner-resolved 2026-07-23). 41 [M] provenance (content not in the app's own voice: UGC, quotes, embeds, promos, code). 42 [M] object-lifecycle (dismissed/dragged/reordered/deleted as a unit, capability existing NOW). 43 [M] interjection (system messages injected into a flow: banners, undo bars). 44 [M] input-region (free-form input surfaces: composer, drop zone, canvas). 45 [M] overlays are already containers — content inside starts flat at rung 0; a box inside a dialog must independently pass a test (the "Mark all unchecked sets" failure). 46 [J] forms structure by labels/proximity/headers, not per-field cards; repeating removable field groups pass #42. 47 [M] contain the minimal unit that passes. 48 [M] passing several tests raises confidence, not chrome. 49 [M] a container near its parent's size separates nothing — unbox. 50 [J] a lone boxed group names its earning test or loses the box. 51 [J] excerpted tappable preview earns containment; same data in place does not — the test is what the user can DO.

**§5 Nesting depth** — 52 [M] count depth from page surface; every common region increments (full-bleed tints and headers/dividers don't). 53 [M] depth 1 = working max for static content; depth 2 = each level independently passes a test (object inside object). 54 [M] depth 3 never — flatten middle / promote to own surface / merge levels. 55 [M] inside a container, subgroup with padding + alignment, not more boxes. 56 [M] inner boundaries step DOWN in strength from parent. 57 [M] elevation never nests (no shadow inside shadow). 58 [M] a container whose sole content is one table/list is the table wearing a frame — unwrap.

**§5a Z-order stacking contract** — 58a [M] one stacking order: content → sticky headers → pinned action bar → bottom nav → FAB → toasts → non-modal sheets → scrim → modal sheets/dialogs → dialog-spawned alerts; no inversions. 58b [M] one scrim per screen, owned by the topmost modal. 58c [M] no layer hides what the task needs; pinned elements reserve their footprint; feedback for a modal action renders inside the modal, never as a toast under its scrim.

**§6 Viewport pricing** — 59 [M] earning tests never change with viewport; the PRICE of a rung does — borderline desktop containers drop to full-bleed rows + dividers on phones (padding is rent: 02 #13 arithmetic). 60 [M] same semantic at different rungs per breakpoint is correct IF decided once in the shared component. 61 [M] when containment dissolves on phones, hit area + per-item identity survive. 62 [J] side-by-side raises the separation requirement; stacked lowers it. 63 [J] density moves the divider threshold, never the container threshold. 64 [M] never add a container to patch a responsive defect (use measure/grid/alignment). 65 [J] one mechanism for every width → prefer tint + whitespace over boxes.

**§7 Consistency** — 66 [M] THE PEER RULE: same-kind groups on one screen sit on the same rung, always (list groups, classify, verify). 67 [J] different sibling treatment must map to a user-discoverable difference. 68 [M] one containment vocabulary per product: each rung means one thing everywhere (mapping table = DS gap, port session). 69 [M] collection members uniform; emphasis via hierarchy, not a different containment species. 70 [M] containment decisions live in shared components, never per-screen markup. 71 [M] same content type on sibling screens = same rung. 72 [J] a screen needing many separator strengths at once is doing too many jobs — check IA first. 73 [M] states keep their group's rung (no box appearing on empty/loading/error).

**§8 Escape hatch operationalized** — 74 [M] every new group starts uncontained (rung ≤4); moving up answers in writing: which rung failed + which test passes + minimal unit at legal depth. 75 [M] "genuinely no other way" = #74 satisfied; cite the test, not the vibe. 76 [M] "cards are banned" is equally wrong — passing tests REQUIRE containment. 77 [M] subtraction test before shipping: hide every border/background in devtools; delete what stays unambiguous. 78 [M] addition test in review: one sentence naming what each boundary separates and why that rung. 79 [M] pills, blobs, outlined chips, framing media ARE containers and take the same tests. 80 [J] torn tint-vs-border after tests → tint on phones; recurring cases go to the decision log.

## Hard-rule digest — canon 06 (rhythm and sectioning)

**§1 Ladder at zone level** — 1 [J] use 01 #11's ladder, climbing from the bottom; alignment shift is a real zone separator. 2 [J] whitespace is the default; a stronger instinct first is wrong. 3 [J] escalate ONLY when: gap would exceed the macro step, zones smear (similar content), or the boundary must survive partial scroll. 4 [M] one boundary one rung; header + whitespace is the one sanctioned pair. 5 [M] same rung for the same boundary kind app-wide. 6 [J] boundary strength matches semantic distance; rank the seams before styling.

**§2 The gap table (THE canonical one)** — 7 [M] same structural relationship = same gap on every screen (highest-leverage rule in the file). 8 [M] item↔item `--sp-2`–`--sp-3` (8–12) · group↔group `--sp-4`–`--sp-5` (16–24) · zone↔zone `--zone-gap` (56 phone → 96 desktop); ≥2× jump between adjacent levels is the non-tunable floor. 9 [M] zone gaps and in-zone gaps are disjoint sets, never overlapping. 10 [J] gaps-only blur audit: structure must survive with text unreadable. 11 [M] the beat holds across the whole scroll, not just viewport one. 12 [M] every gap has an owner token; no ad-hoc padding. 13 [J] density may shift between zones; the beat may not.

**§3 Header grammar** — 14 [M] header binds by asymmetric space: more above than below (above = zone cost, below ≈ one group beat); exception: Home identity lockup takes equal air (owner-scoped, do not "fix"). 15 [M] one header style per structural level; caps ONLY in the two approved eyebrow roles (Q-DS-4). 16 [M] headers quiet, content loud; a header louder than its content inverts hierarchy. 17 [J] omit the header when content self-identifies — everywhere EXCEPT Home, where the title IS the doorway (title + ›, owner 2026-07-20) and always renders. 18 [M] a header carries at most ONE inline utility on its baseline, sentence case; no "See all" on Home. 19 [M] never dangle a header (≥2 content lines beneath before a boundary). 19a [M] large editorial title collapses to a compact pinned bar title on scroll, same choreography everywhere.

**§4 Dividers and tints** — 20 [M] earned dividers are 1px hairline at ~8–15% opacity — the ds.css `--hairline`/`--hairline-2` tokens win. 21 [M] inset = group continues, full-bleed = group ends; encode once in the list component. 22 [J] dividers separate repeated siblings, never unlike zones. 23 [M] tint zone = ONE surface-ramp step, full-bleed (surface-ramp token = DS gap; until it lands, rung-5 tint needs an owner-approved token, not a hex). 24 [M] the moment tint gains radius/border/shadow it IS a card — stop. 25 [J] no alternating-band striping; tint at most 1–2 regions per screen.

**§5 The spine** — 26 [M] one shared left spine at the page margin token, full scroll height — break it and the page falls apart. 27 [M] budget ~2–3 left alignment lines per screen including the spine; off-by-2–6px edges are the worst case. 28 [M] indent = "child of the thing above," one `--sp-4` step; one level phone default, two max anywhere. 29 [M] ragged right except data; compared numerals share one disciplined right edge, tabular figures. 30 [J] align optically at the spine (overhang display glyphs; icons to cap height). 31 [J] full-bleed elements carry the spine's continuation through themselves. 31a [M] RTL mirrors the spine wholesale; numeral columns keep their alignment logic.

**§6 Scale as sectioning** — 32 [J] a decisive scale jump (display ≥ ~2.5–3× body) is itself a boundary; don't also spend a full zone gap on the same seam. 33 [M] the display-numeral + small-caps-label lockup is the standard zone opener; one shared component. 34 [J] one display element per zone, and not every zone gets one — the loudness gradient is information. 35 [M] serif = measured result, sans = interface; never dilute the serif into controls. 36 [J] identical styling reads as same-kind at any distance — never reuse a label style for a non-label.

**§7 Atmosphere and continuity** — 37 [M] one continuous background field; zones never own background rectangles. 38 [J] atmosphere shifts by gradient hand-off over a tall band (≥ zone gap), never a hard seam. 39 [M] hue may encode zone identity; structure must survive greyscale. 40 [J] one or two deliberate bridge elements per screen may stitch zones; never obscuring content or targets. 41 [J] kill the module-stack read: adjacent zones differ in height, density, or layout. 42 [J] one figure per region; everything else is ground — two co-equal focal points = the "cluttered" feeling. 43 [M] any viewport-height slice must reveal which zone you're in (atmosphere, running styles), tested at arbitrary scroll offsets.

**§8 One composition** — 44 [M] one grid per screen, first viewport to last pixel; zones arrange within it, never bring their own margins. 45 [J] one lighting/elevation logic per screen. 46 [J] the 30-second structure test: a viewer names the zones, their starts, their subjects — boxless. 47 [J] compose the whole page (openers, beats, boundary rungs) before styling any zone. 48 [J] a zone resisting cardless treatment gets redesigned (split, scene-encode, summarize, stronger opener) before a container; if earned, it is the screen's ONLY one.

## Hard-rule digest — canon 02 (density and breathing room)

**§1 Density is a decision** — 1 [J] choose density per screen from its task, written down; never inherited from the first pasted component. 2 [J] urgent + repeated → dense; emotional + one-time → airy. 3 [J] compromised hands/attention (mid-set) → bigger targets, bigger data, fewer items. 4 [J] working mode and review mode get two densities switched on mode, not an average. 5 [J] expert-frequency surfaces may run denser; first-run surfaces may not. 6 [J] dense ≠ cramped: remove chrome before shrinking gaps (data-ink). 7 [J] prototype denser-minus-chrome before accepting airier-plus-chrome. Owner ruling 2026-07-23: lean dense on logging surfaces, airy elsewhere — never overdone.

**§2 Glance vs read** — 8 [J] a glance surface answers its one question in <~2s, no scroll or tap. 9 [J] one hero datum an order of magnitude louder — not four peers. 10 [J] read surfaces may be long but every viewport shows front-loaded structure. 11 [J] never mix modes in one viewport; zone glance-first, read-after. 12 [M] compared numbers sit within one eye-span, or summarize as a delta.

**§3 Horizontal budget (THE arithmetic)** — 13 [M] base rent = `--gutter` (24px min at phone widths); beyond it at most ONE additional left inset anywhere — one indent level OR one earned container's padding, never both, never stacked containers; audit edge→first glyph. 14 [J] containers full-bleed-to-margin by default; a narrower one states its meaning or loses the indent. 15 [M] indent step `--sp-4`; one level phone default, two hard max; deeper hierarchy via typography. 16 [M] row text on the content edge; tap target bleeds invisibly full-bleed. 17 [J] leading icons/avatars earn their column (recognition key) or get cut. 18 [M] overflow resolution order: shorten labels → drop tertiary → stack; NEVER shrink below floors, squeeze tracking, or side-scroll the page. 19 [J] steal from the right margin before the left; the left edge is structural.

**§4 Vertical economics** — 20 [M] three gap tiers by rank (06 #8's values), nothing in between. 21 [J] a content-free viewport-height span is a defect; emptiness must look designed. 22 [M] scan-rows run compact: single-line, 4–8px internal padding, 44px floor only because touch; ~8–12 rows per phone viewport. 23 [M] interior headers spend ≤ ~48px (line + below-gap); the space above is the zone seam's cost; big editorial treatment only at the screen's own title, once. 24 [M] row vertical padding ≤ the row's own line-height on working surfaces. 25 [J] trim vertical fat from above the answer first; tail whitespace is cheapest.

**§5 The fold and stuffing** — 26 [M] first viewport = identity + primary datum/action at 320–384×~640–700, no scrolling. 27 [J] long is fine with scent: let a zone peek past the fold; a clean-cut fold reads as page end. 28 [J] overstuffed = scrolling to complete the PRIMARY task; secondary below the fold is design. 29 [M] non-content chrome (headers, tab bars, banners, sticky anything) ≤ ~25% of viewport combined; every sticky element re-earns its permanence. 30 [J] one-datum-per-screen only for capture and celebration; never paginate info needed simultaneously. 31 [M] bounded meaningful sets (today's workout, this week) render whole; infinite mechanics only for unbounded archives. 32 [J] desktop density comes from multi-column zoning, not inflating the phone column. 33 [M] short viewports shed in reverse priority: decoration → secondary data → never the task; input + confirm stay reachable above the keyboard.

**§6 Whitespace as hierarchy** — 34 [J] every gap binds, separates, or is a mistake. 35 [M] gap levels ≥2× apart and obvious at a glance; 14/16/20 reads as sloppy equal. 36 [J] macro (zone) and micro (group) space never trade; compress within groups first, drop content before collapsing zone gaps. 37 [M] whitespace substitutes for dividers; where a gap separates, a line double-charges; the divider is the dense-context discount. 38 [J] emphasis by isolation: at most one isolation-emphasized element per screen. 39 [J] density shifts happen AT zone boundaries and stay constant within them. 40 [M] space above a heading ≥ ~2× the space below. 41 [J] optical density beats mathematical at zone boundaries.

**§7 Measure at screen level** — 42 [M] prose ≤ ~65ch (45–75) even inside wide layouts. 43 [M] at phone width the full content column IS the measure; never sub-inset prose. 44 [J] wide-viewport prose anchors to the reading edge; a skinny centered column in a void = the desktop dead-zone defect. 45 [M] compact rows fit one line at 320px (test long names) or the surface promotes to a uniformly-wrapping layout. 46 [M] display text wraps 1–3 lines max at any width; shrink or shorten.

**§8 Progressive disclosure** — 47 [J] over budget → defer secondary behind a tap, never compress past the floors. 48 [J] frequency-of-need decides what defers, never importance-to-the-business. 49 [M] collapsed state carries truthful scent ("5 sets · top set 225×5 ▸"), never a bare chevron. 50 [M] one disclosure level on phones; tap reveals answer, not another menu. 51 [J] focus+context (expand in place / sheet over context) for mid-task detail; navigate for destinations. 52 [M] never disclose safety-critical or legally-required content. 53 [M] hover-only disclosure forbidden as sole access. 54 [J] working surfaces open with today's section expanded; reading surfaces open with everything visible.
